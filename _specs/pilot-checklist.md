# Pilot masjid checklist

Recruit 3 to 5 pilot masjids BEFORE Step 5. Free for 6 months, then founding price RM59/month locked 24 months.

| Collect | Why | Needed by |
|---|---|---|
| Last 3 monthly statements and the AGM report as submitted | Builds report templates | Before Step 5 |
| The form or letter sent to the state religious authority | Confirms what authorities require | Before Step 12 |
| Current kariah list (Excel or paper) | Tests CSV import | Before Step 5 |
| Khairat rules: fee, who is covered, claim rules | Shapes Step 9 | Before Step 9 |
| One real bank statement CSV (names and account numbers removed if they prefer) | Tests Step 10 | Before Step 10 |
| One-hour interview with Bendahari and Setiausaha | Finds pain points | Before Step 6 |

Interview questions: How do you record Friday collections today? Who counts and who signs? What do you submit, to whom, how often? What goes wrong at month end? Would you pay RM79 a month, and what would you pay?

Get written permission to use their documents for building and testing. Keep real documents out of the git repo (store them outside it).

| Two sample minutes of meeting and the masjid's letterhead (Phase 2) | Shapes the Mesyuarat module and PDF | Before Phase 2 |
