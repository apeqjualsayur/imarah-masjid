# Close month (lock a month)

Once a month has ended and its records have been checked, the Pengerusi **locks** it. A locked month cannot change any more, and official reports for the authorities are produced from locked months only.

- **Lock and unlock:** Pengerusi only.
- **See the month list and checks:** Pengerusi and Bendahari.

## What "locked" means

When a month is locked, **nobody** can add, change or remove a record dated in it: finance entries, Friday collections, khairat payments, bank lines and bank matches. The database itself enforces this, so it applies to every role, including the Imarah team.

If a locked month has a mistake, the Bendahari can **cancel** the entry. The cancellation is dated today (the current month), and the correct entry is recorded in the current month too. The locked month stays as it was.

## Locking a month

1. Go to **Finance > Close month**.
2. Choose the month. Only months that **have ended** can be locked. For example, November can be locked from 1 December.
3. Read the **Checks before locking**:
   - **Friday collections not yet confirmed or waiting for a recount** must be dealt with first. The month cannot be locked while there are any, because once locked they could never be confirmed.
   - **Bank lines not matched**, **difference between bank and ledger** and **non-cash entries not in the bank** are warnings only. Ideally sort them out in **Bank matching** so the difference is RM 0.00.
   - **Counts saved on a phone without internet** and not yet sent cannot be seen here. Ask the counter to send them first.
4. If there are warnings, tick **I have read the warnings above**.
5. Press **Lock month**.

The Pengerusi's name and the time of locking are recorded and shown.

## Unlocking

If a record in a locked month really must change (for example a report needs correcting):

1. Open the month in **Finance > Close month**.
2. Write a **Reason for unlocking**. For example: "Fix an electricity bill recorded in the wrong fund".
3. Press **Unlock**.

The reason and the Pengerusi's name are kept in the **Audit log** and in the month's **Lock history**. After fixing it, lock the month again.

## Reports "no longer valid"

When a month is unlocked, reports already produced for it, and the yearly reports for that year, are marked **No longer valid**. Produce the reports again once the month is locked again. The new report is valid.

## If something goes wrong

- **"A Friday collection in this month is not confirmed."** Open **Friday collections** and confirm, recount or cancel it.
- **"This month has not ended."** Wait until the next month starts.
- **"This month is locked"** when recording. Use a date in a month that is still open, or ask the Pengerusi to unlock it with a reason.
