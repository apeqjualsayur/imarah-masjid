# Audit log

The **Audit log** shows every addition, change and removal in Imarah: who, when, and what changed. The database records it automatically and **nobody can change or delete it**, including the Imarah team.

- **View and filter:** Pengerusi and Bendahari.
- **Export CSV:** Pengerusi.

## Reading the log

Go to **Settings > Audit log**. The newest records are at the top. Each line shows:

- **Action:** Add, Change, Remove, View (for example the Imarah team viewing the masjid list), Email (a receipt was sent), **Lock month** or **Unlock**.
- **Record type:** for example Finance · Finance entry, or Close month and reports · Month.
- **Who and when**, in Malaysian time.
- **What changed**, for example "Amount: RM 50.00 → RM 60.00". Fund, category and user names are shown, not codes.

Personal data (names, addresses, phones, IC, donor names, bank descriptions) is **not kept** in the log. The log only shows "hidden" or "changed (hidden)".

## Filtering

Choose **From** and **To** (dates), **User**, **Record type** or **Action**, then press **Filter**. For example, to see every lock and unlock, choose **Record type: Close month and reports**.

## Export CSV

The Pengerusi can press **Export CSV** to download the log with the same filters (up to 5,000 lines), for the auditor or an AJK meeting.

## If something goes wrong

- **"No access".** Only the Pengerusi and Bendahari can see the audit log.
- **The name says "Not a masjid member".** The action was done by an account outside the masjid, for example the Imarah team changing the plan.
