# Recording receipts and payments

The cash book (**Finance**) keeps every ringgit that comes in and goes out, by fund. Only the **Bendahari** can record. The Pengerusi, Setiausaha and AJK can view. The Imam sees the totals only on the Home page.

## Recording a receipt or payment

1. Go to **Finance** and press **New entry**.
2. Choose **Receive** (money in) or **Pay** (money out).
3. Fill in the **Amount** in ringgit, for example `1,234.50`. At most 2 decimal places.
4. Check the **Date**. Today's date is filled in for you. Dates after today are not allowed: record the entry when the money is actually received or paid (for example, a post-dated cheque is recorded when it clears).
5. Choose the **Fund** and **Category**. The category list follows Receive or Pay.
6. Choose the **Method**: Cash, Bank transfer, DuitNow or Cheque.
7. Fill in the **Reference** (receipt no., cheque no. or bank reference) if needed.
8. For receipts only: fill in **Received from (optional)** for the donor's name on the receipt, and **Donor email (optional)** to send them the receipt PDF. The address is not saved.
9. Fill in a **Note** if needed.
10. If you have one, attach a photo of the receipt or bill (JPG, PNG, WebP) or a PDF, up to 5 MB.
11. Press **Save entry**.

Every receipt of money gets its own receipt number (for example **RC-2026-0001**). See [E-receipts](/bantuan/e-resit) to download, share and send receipts.

**Important:** an entry cannot be changed or deleted once saved. This protects the masjid and the treasurer: every figure can be traced.

## Correcting a mistake (cancelling)

1. Open the wrong entry from the ledger.
2. Under **Cancel this entry**, write the **Reason for cancelling** (for example: wrong amount).
3. Press **Cancel entry** and confirm.
4. A **cancelling entry** dated today takes the original amount back out. The original stays in the ledger, marked **Cancelled**.
5. A new-entry form opens with the same details. Correct what was wrong and press **Save entry**.

The Pengerusi can also cancel an entry, but the correct entry is entered by the Bendahari.

## Reading the ledger

- Choose the month with the arrows above the list.
- Filter by **Fund**, **Category** or **Kind** (in or out), then press **Filter**.
- **Total in** and **Total out** take cancellations into account: a cancelled receipt lowers the total in, rather than adding to the total out.
- Choose one fund only to see the **Opening balance**, the balance after each entry and the **Closing balance**.
- Press **Export CSV** to open the same list in Excel.

## Funds and categories

The Pengerusi and Bendahari manage funds and categories under **Settings > Funds & categories**.

- A **restricted fund** (for example Pembangunan) is only for its purpose. It is marked **Restricted**.
- A fund can only be **archived** when its balance is RM 0.00. Archived funds stay in history and reports, and can be brought back.
- The Asas plan allows 2 active funds.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This month is locked."** The Pengerusi has closed that month. Use a date in an open month, or ask the Pengerusi to unlock the month (a reason is required).
- **"The amount is not valid."** Write it like `1234.50` or `1,234.50`. Do not use more than 2 decimal places.
- **"The date cannot be after today."** Wait until the money is actually received or paid.
- **"This category does not match."** Receipt categories are for Receive only, payment categories for Pay only.
- **"This fund still has a balance."** Move or spend the balance as decided in a meeting first, then archive.
- **"The attachment is larger than 5 MB."** Take the photo at a lower resolution.
