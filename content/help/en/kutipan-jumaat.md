# Friday collections: two people confirm

Every collection is counted by one person and confirmed by another. Once confirmed, a receipt (for example **RC-2026-0001**) is recorded in the ledger automatically. Nobody can confirm their own count, including the Pengerusi and the Bendahari.

- **Counting:** Bendahari or AJK.
- **Confirming:** Pengerusi, Bendahari or AJK, as long as it is not the person who counted.

## 1. Counting

1. On the **Home** page, press **Record collection** (or **Finance > Friday collections > Count collection**).
2. Type the **Amount counted**, for example `1,420.50`. Or press **Count by notes** and enter the number of each note (RM100, RM50, RM20, RM10, RM5, RM1) and the coins total. The total adds itself up.
3. Check the **Date** (today) and **Fund** (Tabung Am).
4. Press **Save count**.

## 2. Confirming

1. The second person opens the collection from **Needs action** on the Home page.
2. The first amount is **hidden**. Count the money yourself and type your amount.
3. Press **Confirm**.
   - **Same amount:** the collection is confirmed and the receipt is recorded in the ledger.
   - **Different amount:** the collection is marked **Needs checking**. Nothing is recorded in the ledger.

## 3. If "Needs checking"

1. Count again together.
2. One person (Bendahari or AJK) opens the collection and enters the agreed amount under **Recount**. That person becomes the counter.
3. **Someone else** confirms as usual.

## No signal at the masjid

If the phone has no signal when you press **Save count**, the count is kept on the phone, marked **Not sent**. It sends itself when there is signal, or press **Send now**. It is never recorded twice.

- This works if the count screen was already open before the signal went. Open **Count collection** while you still have signal.
- Do not sign out before the count has been sent.

## A count entered by mistake

Before it is confirmed, the counter, the Pengerusi or the Bendahari can press **Cancel count** and write a reason. The record stays. After it is confirmed, correct it in the ledger (**Cancel entry** on that receipt).

## If something goes wrong

- **"You cannot confirm your own count."** Ask someone else to confirm.
- **"This count must be recounted first."** The amounts differ. See step 3.
- **"The note breakdown does not match the amount."** Check the number of each note.
- **"This month is locked."** The collection date is in a closed month. Ask the Pengerusi.
