# DuitNow QR and Receive donations

The **Receive donations** screen shows the masjid's DuitNow QR in a large size so donors can scan it with their banking or e-wallet app. It can also be printed as a poster.

Imarah **only displays the QR**. Money goes straight from the donor's bank to the masjid's bank account. Imarah never holds, moves or sees that money, and stores no bank logins.

## Setting up the QR (Pengerusi)

1. Get the masjid's DuitNow QR from the masjid's bank (usually in the banking app, under "DuitNow QR" or "Receive money"). Save a screenshot or picture of it.
2. Go to **Settings > DuitNow QR**.
3. Under **QR picture**, upload that picture. Imarah reads the code and fills in the **QR text** for you. (You can also paste the QR text directly.)
4. Check the **Account name in the QR**. It must be the masjid's bank account name.
5. Tick **I confirm this name is the masjid's bank account** and press **Save QR**.

Imarah checks the QR before saving it: it must be a Malaysian QR, and the code's final check must match. A damaged or mistyped QR is refused, so donors never scan the wrong account. The picture is not kept; only the QR code is.

## QR on receipts

Under **Settings > DuitNow QR**, tick **Show the DuitNow QR on receipts** and press **Save**. The QR is printed at the bottom of the PDF receipt with the words "Derma melalui DuitNow". Cancelled receipts do not show the QR.

## Showing the QR to donors

- Everyone in the masjid can open **Receive donations** from the menu (on a phone: **More > Receive donations**).
- Press **Print poster** to print the QR with the masjid's logo and name, without the app's menus.

## Changing or removing the QR

- **Change:** upload a new QR picture, confirm the name and save.
- **Remove:** press **Remove QR**. The Receive donations screen and receipts stop showing it.

Every change is in the audit log.

## If something goes wrong

- **"The QR could not be read from this picture."** Use a clearer, flat picture, or a screenshot straight from the banking app.
- **"This is not a DuitNow QR."** Make sure it is the QR for *receiving* payments, not another QR (such as a website link).
- **"The QR text is incomplete or mistyped."** Upload the QR picture again instead of typing the text.
- **"This QR is not for Malaysia (RM)."** Use the DuitNow QR from the masjid's Malaysian bank.
