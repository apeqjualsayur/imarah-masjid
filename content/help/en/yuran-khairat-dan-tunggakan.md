# Khairat fees and arrears

The **Khairat** page records each household's khairat membership by year, the fees paid and who is in arrears. Every payment is recorded in the ledger in the khairat fund and gets a numbered receipt.

Khairat is on the **Standard** plan and above.

- **Fee settings:** Pengerusi and Bendahari.
- **Enrol members, record payments, end memberships:** Bendahari and Setiausaha.
- **View the list:** Pengerusi, Bendahari, Setiausaha and AJK.

## 1. Set up this year's fee

1. Go to **Khairat** and press **Set up fees** (or **Fee settings**).
2. Fill in the **Fee**, for example `50.00`.
3. Choose **Who is covered**:
   - **Per household:** one fee for the whole household.
   - **Per person:** the fee times the number of members listed under that household in the Kariah (at least 1).
4. Choose the **Last day to pay**, for example 31 March. After this date, those not paid in full are marked **In arrears**.
5. Choose the **Fund** for payments, usually **Khairat Kematian**.
6. Press **Save settings**.

For a new year, press **Set up fees** for that year. Last year's settings are filled in to start from.

## 2. Enrol members

- **Re-enrol last year's members:** press this button to enrol every member from last year who has not ended. Those already enrolled are not repeated.
- **Enrol new members:** press **Enrol members**, search and tick households, then press **Enrol**. The household must be in the **Kariah** first.

## 3. Record a payment

1. Open the household from the Khairat list.
2. Under **Record a fee payment**, the balance is filled in for you. Change it for a part payment.
3. Choose the **Date** and **Method**. **Received from** on the receipt is the head of household's name, unless you type another.
4. Press **Record payment**.

The payment is recorded in the ledger (khairat fund, Khairat category) and a receipt is issued. Press **View receipt** to download it or share it to WhatsApp. The status and balance update straight away.

A payment cannot be more than that year's balance. To pay last year's arrears, open that year under **Other years** and record the payment there.

## Status

- **Active:** paid in full, or the last day to pay has not passed.
- **In arrears:** the last day to pay has passed and it is not paid in full.
- **Ended:** the membership was ended (for example moved away or passed away). No arrears are counted.

## Arrears list and export

Choose a year, then **Status: In arrears** and press **Filter**. Arrears from earlier years show under the name. Press **Export CSV** to open the list in Excel. The **Khairat** card on the Home page shows this year's summary.

## Correcting a wrong payment

Open the payment in **Finance** and press **Cancel entry** with a reason. The household's amount paid goes down by itself, and its receipt is marked BATAL. Then record the correct payment.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Khairat is on Standard plans and above."** Your masjid is on the Asas plan. The Pengerusi can contact the Imarah team to upgrade.
- **"The amount is more than this year's balance."** Check the balance. A payment for another year is recorded on that year.
- **"Khairat fees for this year are not set up yet."** Ask the Pengerusi or Bendahari to set up the fee first.
- **"This month is locked."** The payment date is in a closed month. Use today's date or ask the Pengerusi.
- **A household is missing from Enrol members:** add it in the **Kariah** first, or it is already enrolled for that year.
