# E-receipts: download, share and email

Every receipt of money gets a numbered receipt automatically, for example **RC-2026-0001**. Numbers follow the year and never repeat, even when a receipt is cancelled. Confirmed Friday collections get numbers from the same sequence. Payments have no receipt.

The receipt PDF shows the masjid's logo and name, the receipt number, date, donor name (if given), fund, method, the amount in figures and in Malay words, and a signature line for the Bendahari.

- **View and download:** Pengerusi, Bendahari, Setiausaha and AJK.
- **Share to WhatsApp and email:** Pengerusi and Bendahari.

## 1. Finding a receipt

1. Go to **Finance > Receipts**.
2. Choose a month with the arrows, or type the number in **Find receipt no.** (for example `0042`).
3. Tap a receipt to open it. You can also tap **View receipt** on an entry in the ledger.

## 2. Downloading the PDF

On the receipt page, tap **Download PDF** to save the file, or **Open PDF** to view or print it.

## 3. Share to WhatsApp

1. Tap **Share to WhatsApp**.
2. WhatsApp opens with a short message in Malay and a link to the receipt. Choose the donor and tap send.
3. The donor can open the link without an account. The link works for **30 days**.

The system never sends messages itself. You press send in WhatsApp.

## 4. Sending by email

- **While recording:** on the **New entry** form, choose **Receive** and fill in **Donor email (optional)**. The receipt is sent after saving.
- **Later:** on the receipt page, fill in **Donor email** and tap **Send email**.

The address is used once to send and **is not saved**. The audit log only notes that the receipt was emailed.

## 5. Donor name

Fill in **Received from (optional)** when recording a receipt. The name is printed on the receipt. Leave it empty if the donor does not want a name printed; the receipt shows "—".

## Cancelled receipts

If an entry is cancelled, its receipt keeps the same number and the PDF copy shows a large **BATAL** mark. A cancelled receipt cannot be shared or emailed. The correct entry gets a new number.

## "Dijana oleh Imarah"

This small line is printed at the bottom of receipts. On paid plans, the Pengerusi can hide it under **Settings > E-receipts**. The Asas plan always shows it.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Pautan resit telah tamat tempoh"** (seen by the donor): open the receipt and tap **Share to WhatsApp** again for a new link.
- **"Email is not set up for this app yet."** Email sending is not switched on. Download the PDF and send it yourself.
- **"The email could not be sent."** Check the address and try again from the receipt page.
- **No logo on the receipt:** upload the logo again under **Settings > Masjid logo**. Without a logo, the masjid's initials are printed.
- **No Share button:** only the Pengerusi and Bendahari can share receipts.
