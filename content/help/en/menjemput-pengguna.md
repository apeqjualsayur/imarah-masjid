# Inviting users

The Pengerusi invites other committee members so they can use Imarah for the same masjid. Each person gets one role (Pengerusi, Bendahari, Setiausaha, AJK or Imam) that decides what they can see and do.

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Users**.
2. Under **Invite a user**, enter the person's email and choose a role.
3. Press **Send invitation**.
4. Press **Copy link** or **Share on WhatsApp** and send the link to that person privately.
5. The person opens the link, enters a name and password, and presses **Accept invitation**.

Invitations not yet accepted show under **Pending invitations**. Press **Cancel** to cancel one.

## Changing a role or deactivating

- Choose a new role next to the person's name and press **Save role**.
- Press **Deactivate** to stop someone signing in. Press **Reactivate** if needed.
- The masjid must always keep at least one active Pengerusi.

## If something goes wrong

- **The link "has expired".** Links work for 7 days. Send a new invitation.
- **"This invitation is for a different email."** The person is signed in with another email. Ask them to sign out and open the link again.
- **"Your account already belongs to a masjid."** One account is for one masjid only. Ask them to use another email.
- **The invitation email did not arrive.** Use **Copy link** and send it on WhatsApp.
