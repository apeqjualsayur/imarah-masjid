# What's new

## November 2026: Cash book

- **Record receipts and payments.** The Bendahari records money in and out under **Finance**, by fund and category, with a photo of the receipt or a PDF attached.
- **Cancel, never delete.** A wrong entry is cancelled with a reason, then the correct one is entered. The original stays, marked "Cancelled".
- **Ledger.** Filter by month, fund, category and kind. Choose one fund for a running balance. Export to CSV.
- **Home dashboard.** Total of all funds, each fund's balance, and this month's money in and out. The Imam sees the totals only.
- **Funds & categories** under Settings: add, rename, mark restricted and archive.
- **Locked months** give a clear message if someone tries to add an entry to them.

## November 2026: Kariah registry

- **Register households.** The Setiausaha can add households and family members under **Kariah**. Search by name, phone or address.
- **Import from Excel.** Download the template, fill it in Excel, and import. The preview shows each row's errors before anything is saved.
- **WhatsApp and PDPA consent** are recorded with their dates.
- **IC number:** only the last 4 digits are stored.
- **Change history** for each household (Pengerusi and Bendahari).

## November 2026: Logo and prayer times

- **Masjid logo.** The Pengerusi can upload a logo (PNG or SVG) under **Settings > Masjid logo**. Without one, the masjid's initials are shown.
- **Real prayer times.** Subuh to Isyak for your masjid's JAKIM zone show at the top. The next prayer is highlighted.
- **Greeting.** The phone app now greets you by name.

## November 2026: Sign-in and users

- **Sign in** with email and password, or with a sign-in link by email.
- **Register a masjid.** New users can register a masjid and become its Pengerusi.
- **Invite users.** The Pengerusi can invite committee members by email or WhatsApp link, change roles and deactivate accounts.
- **Menus by role.** Each role only sees the menus it is allowed to use.
