# What's new

## November 2026: Khairat

- **Yearly khairat fees.** The Pengerusi or Bendahari sets the fee, who is covered (per household or per person), the last day to pay and the fund.
- **Enrol members** from the Kariah, or **re-enrol** all of last year's members with one tap.
- **Record payments** (full or part). Payments go into the khairat fund's ledger with a numbered receipt, and the household's status updates straight away.
- **Status:** Active, In arrears (after the last day to pay) or Ended (with a reason).
- **Arrears list** by year, with arrears from earlier years, and CSV export.
- **Khairat card** on the Home page.
- On Standard plans and above.

## November 2026: E-receipts

- **A receipt number for every receipt.** Every receipt of money gets a number like RC-2026-0001, in the same sequence as Friday collections. Numbers never repeat.
- **Receipt PDF** with the masjid logo, the amount in figures and in Malay words, and a signature line for the Bendahari. Cancelled receipts show BATAL.
- **Finance > Receipts:** find by month or number, download the PDF.
- **Share to WhatsApp:** a short message with a receipt link that works for 30 days. The donor needs no account.
- **Email receipts** to donors (when email is set up). The address is not saved.
- **Donor name** (optional) on the receipt form.
- **Settings > E-receipts:** paid plans can hide "Generated by Imarah".

## November 2026: Friday collections

- **Two people confirm.** The Bendahari or an AJK counts; another person (Pengerusi, Bendahari or AJK) counts for themselves and confirms. Nobody can confirm their own count.
- **Automatic receipts.** Once confirmed, a numbered receipt (for example RC-2026-0001) is recorded in the ledger.
- **Different amounts** are marked "Needs checking" and not recorded until recounted.
- **Note breakdown.** Enter the number of each note and the coins total; the total adds itself up.
- **No signal?** The count is kept on the phone ("Not sent") and sent when there is signal, never recorded twice.
- **Needs action** on the Home page shows collections waiting for you.

## November 2026: Cash book

- **Record receipts and payments.** The Bendahari records money in and out under **Finance**, by fund and category, with a photo of the receipt or a PDF attached.
- **Cancel, never delete.** A wrong entry is cancelled with a reason, then the correct one is entered. The original stays, marked "Cancelled".
- **Ledger.** Filter by month, fund, category and kind. Choose one fund for a running balance. Export to CSV.
- **Home dashboard.** Total of all funds, each fund's balance, and this month's money in and out. The Imam sees the totals only.
- **Funds & categories** under Settings: add, rename, mark restricted and archive.
- **Locked months** give a clear message if someone tries to add an entry to them.

## November 2026: Kariah registry

- **Register households.** The Setiausaha can add households and family members under **Kariah**. Search by name, phone or address.
- **Import from Excel.** Download the template, fill it in Excel, and import. The preview shows each row's errors before anything is saved.
- **WhatsApp and PDPA consent** are recorded with their dates.
- **IC number:** only the last 4 digits are stored.
- **Change history** for each household (Pengerusi and Bendahari).

## November 2026: Logo and prayer times

- **Masjid logo.** The Pengerusi can upload a logo (PNG or SVG) under **Settings > Masjid logo**. Without one, the masjid's initials are shown.
- **Real prayer times.** Subuh to Isyak for your masjid's JAKIM zone show at the top. The next prayer is highlighted.
- **Greeting.** The phone app now greets you by name.

## November 2026: Sign-in and users

- **Sign in** with email and password, or with a sign-in link by email.
- **Register a masjid.** New users can register a masjid and become its Pengerusi.
- **Invite users.** The Pengerusi can invite committee members by email or WhatsApp link, change roles and deactivate accounts.
- **Menus by role.** Each role only sees the menus it is allowed to use.
